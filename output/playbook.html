Grounded in: live comlink roster + swgoh.gg GAC meta (5v5 S80 / 3v3 S81, Hold% and Win%) + /gac/ship-counters battle data. Every unit owned and G13+; no unit repeats within a mode.
How the squads were chosen. Not "best Hold% first". A GAC round is decided on net banners, so the board maximises Σ P(defense holds) + Σ P(offense clears) as one exact optimisation over every squad you can field, with no unit used twice. That is what stops an 18%-hold wall from eating the units of a 90%-win attacker.
Defense vs offense split of the 9 GLs: JMK, JML, SEE and SLKR are attack-only (they defend poorly and a defensive placement strands their support). The rest are placed by the optimiser.
⚠ Read this before you trust any percentage: what is rented and what is owned
Every Hold%/Win% on this page was measured last season, under the datacrons that were live then. Four sets are live right now (swgoh.gg/datacrons, read 2026-08-05). Only the faction and role tiers tell squads apart — the Light Side / Dark Side tiers apply to everyone:

Finding 1 — the faction tiers. When set 30 lapses, no live set grants a Sith or Galactic Republic faction bonus. They keep the Light/Dark Side tier (set 33 carries it) and set 32's role tiers, so it is one tier lost, not a blackout.

Finding 2 — the one that actually moved this board: FOCUSED datacrons. Set 30 also carries four character-specific datacrons, verified from its variant list: Cassian Andor (Undercover) · Darth Revan · Dedra Meero · Luminara Unduli — all expiring 2026-08-06, non-rerollable. A focused cron keys off one named character, so no faction or role heuristic can see it. Three of those four sit on this roster's board.

How much of each rate is rented — measured, not guessed. swgoh.gg's squad tier lists publish, per leader, the rate from the battles where no L9 datacron applied. That is a real counterfactual, and ratio = baseline ÷ headline says how much of a rate is owned. Worked example: Cassian Andor (Undercover) reads 25.1% in 5v5 and 28.9% in 3v3, but his no-datacron baselines are 12.4% and 7.8% — ratios of 0.49 and 0.27, agreeing across two independent formats. His wall was dropped from the 5v5 board as a result. By contrast Queen Amidala's baseline (30.8%) is HIGHER than her headline (25.3%), so her wall was promoted.

Applied to defense only, on evidence. Measured offense ratios all land between 0.87 and 1.04 — an attacker already winning 90% is not being carried by a datacron. Defense ratios run 0.35 to 1.15. Defense is also the side you cannot adapt: it is set once and then attacked by whoever turns up. (An earlier faction-tag version of this correction was tried and thrown away — it could not see focused crons at all, and its haircut scrambled the board rather than ranking it.)

One more population caveat, reported not priced in. The lineup-level source (/gac/squads/) is all-league and does not honour a league filter — it pools Carbonite players with Kyber ones, and weaker attackers make every wall look better than it will for you. The leader-level tier lists can be filtered to Kyber-D1, and where that says a wall does materially worse it is flagged ⚠ Kyber-D1 in the Why column below. It is not applied as a multiplier because the ratio confounds population skill with build mix: the all-league "Rey" 5v5 row averages 531 different builds and reads 9.6%, the Kyber-D1 row averages 57 and reads 20.9% — Rey does not improve against better players, the low-league average is just full of junk builds. One exception is applied, where two independent lines of evidence agree: the 3v3 Rey / Ben Solo / Luminara wall reads 10.3% at Kyber-D1 (n=5,222) and loses Luminara's focused datacron tomorrow. It was demoted from #2 to #13.
